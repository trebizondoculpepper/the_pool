# Explicit facet coverage for registered kinds — issue #43

Research for the owner, 2026-11-07. This answers [issue #43](https://github.com/trebizondoculpepper/the_pool/issues/43) before any validation or trait change. Facets and kind traits describe permanent capabilities; condition and operation belong to a live instance. The audit below used the 124 registered kinds at `833611a2`, expanded their composite traits with `expand`, and inspected unlike kinds with `npm run audit -- show`.

## Answer

Do **not** require a positive trait in every facet. The current facets are independent questions, many of which genuinely do not apply. Requiring a marker such as `not-surface` or `no-volume` would add vocabulary with no gameplay effect and violate the project's trait rule. Require answers only for facets with a general gameplay obligation, and make an audit able to distinguish **present**, **derived**, **not applicable** and **unresolved**. That is explicit coverage of the audit's questions, without pretending every kind has every capability.

The current rules already do much of this: a kind that needs floor, wall or ceiling mounting must have a support trait; an in-line, terminal, holdable, luggable, run-like or water-body kind can be supported by its connection, hand or room instead (`needsSupport` in `src/game/plant/traits.ts`). Service is derived from model ports, never hand-authored as a kind trait. The missing validation worth fixing is **handling**: `FACET_RULES.handling` says `exactly: 1`, but `primitiveProblems` currently rejects only _more than_ one, and `office-shelf` is the one kind with none. Its permanent mounting and gameplay suggest `fixed`, subject to checking its rack interaction before using the audit command.

I found no all-facets positive-trait requirement in these primary systems. The proposed universal rule is therefore a **local design choice**, not established engine practice. That is an inference from the surveyed systems, not a claim that no game anywhere uses one.

## Current coverage

The 124-kind audit found these counts of kinds without a primitive in each facet: form 6; volume 121; surface 115; support 25; connection 106; handling 1; use 4; role 107; service 124; decorative 120. These are **not** 124 errors. All 25 kinds without a support primitive are exempt under `needsSupport`; no kind that currently needs mounting lacks it. All four kinds without a use primitive are decorative. All 124 lack a service primitive because service is derived from model ports. The six without form are four ladder kinds plus the pool and footbath basins. `office-shelf` is the sole missing handling classification.

## Recommended validation and audit

1. Keep the current `atMost` rules and the conditional support rule. Make the handling rule genuinely require one handling primitive for a non-decorative classified kind, after classifying `office-shelf` through `npm run audit`. Decorative kinds may still be `fixed` where that affects gameplay, but no filler trait is required for a purely inert item.
2. Keep volume, surface, connection, role and decorative optional. A positive trait is required only when the corresponding capability is claimed or another trait's `needs` rule requires it. Do not add an all-facets error.
3. Show audit coverage as `present` (trait and expansion path), `derived` (for service, with its port evidence), `not applicable` (with the applicability rule) or `unresolved` (a real missing answer). This is a report about the model, not ten new kind fields. Missing source evidence remains `unresolved`, not `none found` interpreted as proof of no service.
4. Use [description research #44](object-description-order.md) as a secondary check. If a truthful sentence lacks a stable fact, ask whether an existing kind trait, authored specification or geometry supplies it. If the missing fact can change during play, seek a world reader instead.

Counterexamples to a universal rule are the held software box's support, a sign's volume, a pipe's floor mounting, and the water body's operational colour. No trait additions or kind edits were made in this research.
