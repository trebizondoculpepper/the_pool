## Scope and source of truth

Publish one versioned, pure, read-only process snapshot for all four pool circuits, the two CO₂ installations, the wet-hall water system and the LTHW ring. Read the current `World` and existing model helpers; do not add invented sensors, modify the physical model, save AI-specific state, or expose action dispatch. Name each water body and its location: balance tank, and the main pool's far-side measured cell. Where the model has three pool cells, identify that the main-pool reading is the far side, not a whole-pool average. Give pH, total alkalinity (mg/L as CaCO₃), free and combined chlorine (mg/L as Cl₂), calcium hardness (mg/L as CaCO₃), temperature (°C), volume (m³) and level where each is actually modelled. Mark unavailable fields explicitly. Report circulation flow (m³/h), pool level, tank fill, make-up/drain state, and the major wet-hall flows and pit levels from the world.

For Plants 1 and 2, expose the Trio's measured pH, target band, selector, CO₂ channel demand and release, the room doser's mode, asked and delivered g/h, fault, valve and override, the shared supply isolation and master state, wall-port position, and line/port pressure in bar gauge. Distinguish controller demand, panel request and actual delivery. Describe the controller's saturation only from its actual output and limits, never from a guessed cause. The LTHW section gives header flow/return temperatures, set point, pumps, supply/demand/delivery kW, sequencer enable/call/fault state and each pool's temperature, target and heat input.

Keep a bounded, browser-session history sampled by **simulation time**. It must not grow during pause or a repeated world timestamp, and must reset if world time goes backwards (New Game/load). Use samples spanning several simulated minutes for pH and alkalinity slopes, with start/end values and timestamps; mark a trend unavailable until enough simulation time has elapsed. A trend is evidence, not a conclusion that a treatment is succeeding. There is no long-term history in `World`, so a reload starts a new observation window.
