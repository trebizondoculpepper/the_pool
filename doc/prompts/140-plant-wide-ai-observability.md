# 140 — Plant-wide AI observability and causal diagnosis

The owner, 2026-11-02: ChatGPT is an external, read-only engineering assistant for the running game. The browser diagnostics of [134](134-browser-chatbot-context.md) and the water and CO₂ process readings of [139](139-browser-plant-process-state.md) have worked in the owner's ChatGPT workflow: it can read current measurements and recent trends, and distinguish a main pool from its balance tank. Give it the same evidence across the rest of the plant, so it can investigate a deliberately introduced fault without being told what changed. The test is a technically grounded account of **cause and effect**, not a longer list of alarms or a confident guess from an event log.

## Outcome

From the live browser page alone, ChatGPT can say what is happening now, what was requested, what actually happened, what limits or interlocks applied, which upstream source and downstream consumers matter, what changed over the last five simulated minutes, and which parts remain unknown. It names stable equipment, controller and simulation-node refs, measurements with units and simulation timestamps, and the model rule or connection supporting each causal link. It distinguishes an observed condition from a derived consequence and a hypothesis. An old event is historical evidence, never proof that an alarm or trip is still present.

The assistant must be able to answer, with evidence, questions such as why a boiler is firing, whether the LTHW ring meets current heat demand, what followed a heating-circuit isolation, why a pump stopped, which feeder supplies it, whether a protective device tripped, whether a controller is asking more than its actuator delivers, which downstream systems changed, and whether the plant is recovering after an intervention. It must also be able to say “the model has no reading or connection for that conclusion” when appropriate.

## Existing foundations and boundary

Reuse the one saved `World`, the model's port-addressed blocks and enhanced networks, the real equipment and alarm readers in 134, the process snapshot and simulation-time trends in 139, the recent event log, the control descriptors of 131, the room/item audits of 129/130, and 138's pure simulation graph and direct-neighbour relations. The 138 graph is a development audit of model relationships; validate every edge used for live diagnosis against the runtime world, its schematic or its actual source binding. Its deferred impact search is a useful starting hook, not proof that every indirect consequence is already known.

Use the **one shared, versioned diagnostic fact and edge contract** defined under 141, with one record per modelled engineering object or process node, not a parallel simulation or a second set of hand-written statuses. A record identifies its stable ref, room and system, model source, observation time, and availability. It separates (1) configured intent and setpoint, (2) current command and mode, (3) actual actuator or machine state, (4) measured process value, (5) constraints, trip and interlock state, (6) recent change, and (7) incoming and outgoing connections. Each field carries its unit and whether it is measured, directly stored, derived from a documented model rule, or unavailable. An unavailable field is not OFF, zero, normal or healthy. 140's domain readers populate this shared contract and consume 141's purpose-typed bounded walk; they must not add a second traversal or provenance scheme.

No browser-facing method may expose a mutable world object, raw save, action dispatch, player identity, puzzle solution or spoiler. The browser assistant gets facts and evidence, never a control channel. No new sensor, physical process, alarm or dependency may be fabricated to make a report look complete. A gap in the game model is a coverage finding and a separate model task.

Cross-domain joins are essential: a breaker feeds a motor, the motor drives a pump, the pump moves water through valves and filters, a sensor reports a value to a controller, a controller commands an actuator, and a boiler supplies heat to the LTHW ring and its consumers. Show the path and each edge's source. A node's direct upstream/downstream neighbours and a bounded indirect impact search must be queryable by ref. Cycle handling, limits and direction must make the search finite; the result says which nodes have a proven live path, which only have a schematic relationship, and which cannot be resolved.

## Time and causality

Extend 139's bounded browser-session history to the process values needed for these domains, using simulation time rather than wall time. Keep at least a five-simulated-minute comparison where the model and session permit it, with start and end values, units, rate of change and sample count. Reset the history on New Game or save replacement; pause must not create a slope. A newly opened tab or insufficient history reports that limitation. Keep the event log alongside current state, with a clear label for past actions and transitions. The diagnostic layer may correlate an intervention with a later response, but must not turn temporal coincidence into a proven cause without a model path or rule.

For each actual discrepancy (commanded versus delivered, setpoint versus measured, supply versus demand, expected versus observed flow), provide a compact **evidence chain**: the command and source, the current actuator and process readings, the applicable interlock or limit from the model, upstream/downstream refs and any relevant recent transition. Rule-based explanations may reuse the controller's own predicates, such as the Wash-O-Tron's refusal rules. Where several causes fit, report candidates and the missing discriminator; do not choose one by guess. “What happens if I isolate …?” may be answered from a documented dependency path and stated as a prediction; any numerical counterfactual requires a separate, isolated model replay that does not alter the saved game, and must be labelled as a prediction rather than an observed result.

## Browser surface

The owner has confirmed that ChatGPT successfully reads the current page's semantic diagnostics. Extend that same enabled/off-switch-controlled surface: an always-present, concise facility index and current anomalies, plus domain/system sections with the essential numbers, stable refs, command-versus-actual pairs, five-minute changes and causal paths as ordinary accessible DOM text. Keep the detailed, searchable and copyable `#plant-context-report` as the full evidence view. A user should not have to run JavaScript, open the developer console, explain the fault or navigate to the affected room. The existing `window.__poolDiagnostics` API gains bounded indexed queries for tools that can use it, but JavaScript methods alone do not satisfy this mission. Avoid a giant unstructured world dump, repeated screen-reader announcements and per-frame DOM churn; update derived content at most once a second and only when its displayed values change.

The page must make coverage visible: a per-domain and per-system count of modelled objects, objects with live state, measurements, commands, connections, trend history and cause predicates; list missing readers and broken or unverified graph joins by ref. Keep the compact page text useful within the browser assistant's actual extraction limits. Test the text the owner’s ChatGPT side panel receives, including whether closed `details`, `display:none`, visually hidden text and late sections are omitted or truncated. Put the facility index, anomalies and off-normal list before detailed bundles and the atlas. Keep ordering stable so two reads can be compared. The report must do no diagnostic work when the AI-context switch is off; when on, update at most once a second and only on displayed change, with a frame-time check.

The owner’s broad “anywhere in the plant” criterion is measured against a published inventory of modelled, faultable engineering systems; unmodelled equipment cannot be silently counted as diagnosed.

## Acceptance matrix

- **Boiler and heat:** impose a heat demand, lock out or disable a boiler, operate a modelled gas ECV/SSOV or ring pump, or change the sequencer setpoint. ChatGPT identifies the cascade's call, stage-up/stage-down hold and minimum-run/off timers where relevant, boiler response, LTHW readings, consumer demand and delivered kW, and whether demand is met. The current heat consumers have no circuit-isolating valves; that fault trial is a model gap, not an acceptance case until built.
- **Electrical to hydraulic:** trip the modelled incomer breaker or change a modelled feeder/isolator supplying a circulation pump. ChatGPT traces supply → feeder/protection → starter/motor → pump → water flow, distinguishes a STOP command from lost power and a trip, and names other affected loads through verified paths. Per-load breakers, RCDs and motor current are coverage gaps until modelled.
- **Controller mismatch:** set a controller to request output while its actuator is OFF, isolated, saturated or faulted. ChatGPT quotes the setpoint, measured input, requested output, actual response and interlock, and identifies the missing response as the discrepancy.
- **Air handling:** stop a fan or change a modelled damper/control mode. ChatGPT identifies the command and actual state, airflow if modelled, hall temperature/humidity trend, heating/cooling demand and the power or control reason where known.
- **Filtration and backwash:** obstruct flow or inhibit a wash through a valve, low circulation, filter state or pit capacity. ChatGPT reports the current hydraulic path, relevant levels/flows/differential pressures, Wash-O-Tron state and its actual refusal predicate.
- **Recovery:** clear each fault and advance five simulated minutes, including a pause. ChatGPT compares timestamped before/after measurements and current state, says whether the affected process is recovering, and does not attribute the pause or an old alarm event to a current fault.

For every case, the structured query, accessible text, full report and game instrument must agree at the same world time. Tests run with the player in a different room from the affected equipment. An unknown measurement or graph edge is explicit in all surfaces. A no-fault baseline must not be described as failed. Cover ambiguous multi-fault cases and report multiple supported causes rather than a false single answer.
