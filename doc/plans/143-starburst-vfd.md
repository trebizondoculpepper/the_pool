# 143: A 14-segment VFD, and the Wash-O-Tron's console on it

The plan and record for mission 143 (`doc/prompts/143-starburst-vfd.md`; the owner, 2026-11-02, "Do 143 now"). Research in `doc/research/starburst-vfd.md`.

## What was built

- **The glyph table** (`src/toolkit/components/industrial-control/logic/starburst-font.ts`, tested beside it): the fourteen segments and decimal point by their conventional names (A–F, G1, G2, H, J, K, L, M, N, DP); a glyph for A–Z, 0–9, space and the punctuation `- + / \ * < > _ ' " . , = ( ) [ ] % ? ! # $ & @ ^ |` and the backquote; lower case folded to upper case, as the period's 64-character decoders did (the Wash-O-Tron shows `h`, `m3` and `s`). `starburst(c)` gives a character's segments or null; `canDraw` and `undrawable` check a string. There is no colon. `segmentStroke` places each segment in its cell as a bar with its ends drawn back, so no two segments touch. The tests check every letter and digit has a glyph of its own (5 and S differ: 5 takes the lower-right diagonal), and that M, W, K, X, V, N and Z are drawn on their diagonals.
- **The display** (`src/toolkit/components/industrial-control/views/starburst-vfd.ts`, `createStarburstVfd`, a sibling of `createVfd`): `rows` by `columns` characters, 5 mm high, 0.56 of their height wide at a pitch of 1.45 widths, rows 1.5 heights apart; 2 × 20 is 86.2 × 17.5 mm of glass (the LCD it replaces was 79 × 14 mm). A `vfd` canvas (`display-reach.ts`) at 8 px/mm, 690 × 140. What never changes is drawn once into two offscreen layers: the anode plate with every segment's ghost, and the grid mesh with each character's frame. A redraw is the plate, the lit segments as one path stroked three times (a wide faint glow under them, the stroke, a thin hot core), and the mesh over them: six canvas calls. It redraws and uploads only when its text, power, brightness or the power-on test changes (its key compared before any drawing). Its housing is a bezel round a 6 mm recess: the anode plate at the back (unlit, self-lit material), two filaments a row 2 mm in front of it, tinted, slightly reflective glass at the front. `show(lines, on)` as `TextDisplay`; `setBrightness(0.25–1)`; `update(delta)` ends the 0.8 s power-on test, every segment lit.
- **On dev0** (`src/toolkit/gallery/rooms/starburst-vfd-board.ts`, south wall at x = 1.0, between the jog dial and the distribution board): the display at twice life size with POWER, DIM and PAGE; four pages: the alphabet and digits, the punctuation with M W K X V N Z, a backwash step timer counting (`02-30/03-00`), and a running clock with changing messages. Look links: `?room=dev0&look=14-segment-vfd` (the board) and `look=14-segment-vfd-display`.
- **The Wash-O-Tron** (`src/toolkit/components/plant/views/wash-o-tron.ts`): `createCharacterLcd` replaced by `createStarburstVfd`, two lines of 20, in the same place; named for look links (`look=wash-o-tron-display`). Nothing else on the console changed. `washDisplay` writes step times `mm-ss` and shows the event log's `hh:mm` as `hh-mm` (the log itself is unchanged); `wash-o-tron-display.test.ts` checks the dash and that every line of every view is drawable.

## Findings for the owner

- **Period clash:** the Wash-O-Tron is described as the filter maker's retrofit controller of about 1996, in a Penfold panel with a pale blue bubble-printed leisure fascia; an early-1980s starburst VFD on it is some fifteen years older than the controller around it. It reads as a deliberate anachronism, which the game allows; the console was not redesigned.
- **Look links in `plant_pool_02`** put the camera at y = 1 and look nearly straight down (`pitch -83`, 0/9 rays) at the Wash-O-Tron and its parts, whose floor is well below y = 0; and `look=lidoluxe-wash-o-tron-filter-wash-controller` is refused though the console is marked with that name.
