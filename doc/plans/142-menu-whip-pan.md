# 142 plan: the Esc menu keeps arrows and WASD off the game, and whip-pans between controls when zoomed

The plan for [mission 142](../prompts/142-menu-whip-pan.md) (the owner, 2026-11-02: "When in the esc menu, the arrow keys, and wsad should not affect the game. When zoomed in it should flick (whip-pan) the camera to a control in that direction (if one exists)."). Written 2026-11-02, from the code at `259e29a4` and a headless-Chrome reproduction on the shared dev server. Not implemented: the mission waits for the owner's go.

Reviewed by Codex (read-only, 2026-11-02); its valid findings are folded in: duplicate target ids on the DISPLAY module, keyup and modifier handling across opening and closing, click rules while zoomed, the repeat timer's edge cases, and a sharper browser acceptance.

## Part 1: the leak

### What was checked and found sound

- The walker: `inputHeld()` (`src/toolkit/controls/input/first-person.ts:75`) is true while `menuOpen` is set; `keydown` returns at once on it (`first-person.ts:902`); every frame clears `keys` and `tapped` (`first-person.ts:1622-1628`) and multiplies walking, flight lift and roll by `still = 0` (`first-person.ts:1633`); the gamepad reader returns idle and marks the held buttons as already pressed (`first-person.ts:1243-1249`); `use` refuses (`first-person.ts:364`); mouse look needs the pointer captured, which `openMenu` frees (`menu-overlay.ts:358`).
- The reader: `menu-input.ts` listens on the window in the capture phase; a key a menu takes is stopped with `preventDefault` and `stopImmediatePropagation` (`menu-input.ts:51-57`), and while the overlay is modal every other key is stopped too (`menu-input.ts:59`, `modal: () => open` at `menu-overlay.ts:548`), so `first-person.ts`'s document listeners (`first-person.ts:1199-1200`) never hear it.
- Other key listeners (`grep "'keydown'" src`): the console (`console-view.ts:285`, only its own key), the plain pause menu (`pause-menu.ts:472-473`, closed while the panel is on), the title (`title-screen.ts:112`, `covered()` while a menu is open), the shared plant layout's G and R (`rendering/shared-plant-layout.ts:467`, its own page only), and the audio unlock (`part-audio.ts:150`). None reads the arrows or WASD in a room behind the menu.
- Reproduction (headless Chrome, `dev0`, real key events through the DevTools protocol, the pose read from `pool-plant:last-location` after a `pagehide`): W held for 0.6 s with no menu walks 1.3 m; with the menu open and the focus on Resume, each of the eight keys held 0.7 s with auto-repeat leaves the pose unchanged. The walker is not the leak.

### The leak: the panel's accessible copy hands the keys to the browser

The overlay keeps a visually hidden DOM copy of the panel for screen readers (`src/toolkit/menus/views/dom-mirror.ts`), and moves the document's focus onto the copy of the focused control (`menu-overlay.ts:506`, `dom-mirror.ts:192`). The copy of a knob (a pot: field of view, master volume, render quality, sensitivity) is an `<input type="range">` (`dom-mirror.ts:114-136`), and the copy of a selector (graphics, on-screen controls) a `<select>` (`dom-mirror.ts:137-156`).

1. **The reader stands aside on a range input** (the main leak). `typing()` (`menu-input.ts:29-32`) counts any `HTMLInputElement` as a field being typed into, and `keydown` returns before anything else when it is (`menu-input.ts:47`): no menu intent, no modal hold, no `preventDefault`. So once the focus rests on a knob (ArrowDown from Resume lands on Field of view), every key goes past the menu: the browser's own slider handling takes all four arrows (and Home, End, PageUp, PageDown) and steps the setting through the copy's `input` event (`dom-mirror.ts:121-123`, `updateSettings`). Reproduced: with the focus on Field of view, ArrowLeft held took the field of view from 70 to 40, ArrowRight held brought it back, and a single ArrowRight took it from 70 to 54; the room behind visibly zooms. Up and Down no longer move the focus (it is trapped on the knob), and Esc no longer closes the menu (reproduced: the panel stayed open). W, A, S and D are not held back either (`menu-input.ts:59` is never reached), so they reach every later listener; the walker ignores them only because of its own `inputHeld()` and `typing` checks.
2. **The modal hold does not prevent the browser's default** (latent). For a key no menu takes, `menu-input.ts:59` calls `stopImmediatePropagation` but not `preventDefault`, so the focused copy still does its native thing: a `<select>` takes letters as type-ahead (A on On-screen controls would choose "Always"; not reproduced in headless Chrome, whose synthetic keys did not drive type-ahead), a range input takes Home and End.
3. **By design, but what the owner may be seeing:** Left and Right on a focused knob or selector turn it (`menuStep`, `menu-navigation.ts:228-235`), and every auto-repeated keydown is a turn, so a held arrow sweeps the field of view or the graphics preset and the game behind changes. That is the menu working its own control, which is what it is for; whether Left and Right should keep doing it is an open question below.
4. **W, A, S and D are not menu keys at all.** `keyIntent` maps only the arrows (`menu-navigation.ts:41-68`; the test at `menu-navigation.test.ts:70` pins `keyIntent('w')` to null). The brief's "the arrows and WASD keep working the menu's focus as they do now" is not so today: WASD do nothing in the menu.

No other menu panel opens without `menuOpen`: the plain pause menu sets `paused` (`pause-menu.ts:457`), which `inputHeld()` also counts; the dry peek and the noclip camera are moved by the same walker, held the same way.

### The fix, at the source

In `src/toolkit/controls/logic/menu-navigation.ts` (pure) and `input/menu-input.ts`:

- **Typing means text entry only.** A pure `isTextEntry({ tag, type, editable })`: a `textarea`, an editable element, or an `input` whose type takes text (`text`, `search`, `email`, `url`, `tel`, `password`, `number`, and no type). A range, checkbox, radio, button or `select` is not. `typing()` in `menu-input.ts` becomes a thin wrapper reading the event target into it. The console's input stays a text field, so its keys stay its own.
- **One decision for every key**, pure: `menuKeyFate({ intent, modal, typing, modifiers })` returns `'menu'` (offer the intent to the listeners), `'hold'` (a modal menu has the keys: stop it, and prevent the browser's default when no Ctrl, Cmd or Alt is held, so browser shortcuts such as reload still work) or `'pass'`. While modal, no key ever reaches the game's listeners, modified or not (stopped as now); only an unmodified key also has its default prevented. A text field's keys (`typing`) are left to the field, and are still stopped from reaching the game while modal. `menu-input.ts`'s keydown and keyup follow it.
- **Keyups.** A key whose keydown a menu took or held is remembered (the `taken` set, `menu-input.ts:26`, extended to held keys) and its keyup is stopped too, whatever the modal state or modifiers are by then. A walk key held down as the menu opens: the walker's frame already clears `keys` while `inputHeld()` (`first-person.ts:1622-1628`), so it does not walk on; held through the menu's closing, its next auto-repeat keydown reaches the walker, which walks while it is held, as a fresh press would. Tests cover open-while-held and close-while-held.
- **W, A, S and D become direction keys in menus**, matched by `event.code` (`KeyW`, `KeyA`, `KeyS`, `KeyD`, as the walker matches them, so they sit under the left hand on any layout): `keyIntent(key, shift, code)`. Unzoomed they mean what the arrows mean. They are still never taken while a text field has the focus (the console's input, the dev panel's search box).
- The mirror's copies keep working for a screen reader's own commands (its virtual cursor drives them with clicks and value changes, not keys); a keyboard user's arrows now go through the menu, which turns the same setting.

### Tests

- `menu-navigation.test.ts`: `isTextEntry` for each input type and tag (range, select, button: not text; text, search, textarea, contenteditable: text); `menuKeyFate` for each of the eight keys (by key and by code) with the menu modal and the focus on a button, a range, a select and nothing: never `'pass'`, and `'hold'` keys prevented unless modified; a text field's keys pass while not modal; `keyIntent` for the WASD codes (the old `keyIntent('w')` null expectation changes); a key taken or held on keydown has its keyup held whatever the modal state by then (the `taken` bookkeeping made a pure set of rules in the same module).
- `menu-input.ts` stays thin (DOM only); no jsdom test is added for it.
- Browser check (stage 1's acceptance, recorded in this plan when done): the reproduction script below, extended, opens `dev0`, opens the menu with Esc, and for each of the focus on Resume, on Field of view (a range copy), on Graphics (a select copy) and on Noclip holds each of the eight keys for 0.7 s with auto-repeat, then reads the walker's pose (position, yaw, pitch, zoom, crouch, noclip), `pool-plant:settings`, and the world's saved state with its time-driven parts removed (it runs on behind the menu: `time`, `savedAt` and whatever the simulation steps by itself are compared against a second run of the same length with no keys pressed, not against zero change), and finally presses Esc and checks the menu closed. Before the fix it shows the field of view moving and Esc failing on the range; after it, the pose and settings are unchanged, except where Left and Right turn the focused knob by the menu's own rule (stage 1 records which).
- The reproduction lives in the scratchpad for now (`repro.mjs`, `repro3.mjs`, driving Chrome over the DevTools protocol as `scripts/look.ts` does); stage 1 commits it as `scripts/menu-keys-check.ts` so it can be rerun, and adds it to `doc/agent-tooling.md`.

## Part 2: the whip-pan

### What there is

- **A control** in the menu's model is a `MenuTarget` (`menu-navigation.ts:32-38`): `id`, `x`, `y` in panel metres from the panel's top left, y down, and `turns` for a knob or selector. The settings panel makes one per module (`settings-panel-view.ts:576-597`, `focusable`): its `id` is the module's title, its point the module's centre plus the part's offset. Blanks have no target. **The ids are not unique** (found in review): the DISPLAY module holds two switches side by side, MAX RESOLUTION and SCALING MODE (`settings-panel.ts:381-388`, `settings-panel-view.ts:806-845`), and both targets get the id `DISPLAY`. So `focus` rings both, `work` and `menuStep` find only the first, and SCALING MODE cannot be reached by keys or pad today; the mirror has no copy of either switch (`dom-mirror.ts` has no `'switch'` case). Part 2 depends on same-plate moves, so it fixes this first.
- **A plate** is a `PlateBox` (`menu-overlay.ts:36`), a `ModuleBox` or `BlankBox` of the layout (`panel-layout.ts:74`, `layout.modules`, `layout.blanks`) in the same panel metres. A control's plate is the module whose title is its `id` (or, more robustly, the module whose box contains its point); `plateAt` (`menu-overlay.ts:415-434`) finds a plate under the pointer the same way by box.
- **The zoom** (`menu-overlay.ts:151-181`, `203-214`): `zoomTo(box)` eases `view` from where it is (`view ?? wholeView()`) to `plateView(box, layout, innerWidth, innerHeight)` (`iso-view.ts:140`) over `ZOOM_SECONDS` = 0.45 s (`iso-view.ts:125`) with `viewBetween` (`iso-view.ts:172`, cubic `easeInOut`, the scale eased in proportion). It already restarts from the present view if called mid-ease, and skips if `box === zoomed` (an identity test). Only a click on a plate's bare face zooms (`menu-overlay.ts:448-453`); Esc, B zoom out (`menu-overlay.ts:537`).
- **Focus moves** go through `menuStep` (`menu-navigation.ts:208-249`) and `showFocus` (`menu-overlay.ts:503-515`), which rings the control, moves the mirror's focus, and scrolls the unzoomed panel to it. A pointer press hides the ring (`menu-overlay.ts:440`) but leaves `focused` as it was, so after a click-zoom the arrows would move from an old, possibly distant, focus.
- **Inputs:** keyboard through `keyIntent` on keydown, every auto-repeat included (the operating system's rate, typically 30 a second after half a second); the pad's buttons (`MENU_PAD`, d-pad 12-15) once per press with no repeat; the left stick as a direction once as it leans past `STICK_LEAN` 0.6 and again every `STICK_REPEAT` 0.35 s (`menu-navigation.ts:84-134`). The menu has no keyboard repeat rate of its own today; the stick's 0.35 s is the only one.

### Design

**Unique control ids first.** `focusable` gives each target its own id, the module's title alone where it has one control (so nothing that names them now changes) and `TITLE/legend` for each of a module's several switches (`DISPLAY/MAX RESOLUTION`, `DISPLAY/SCALING MODE`), and a `plate` field naming its module, so a control's plate is looked up by name, not guessed. `MenuTarget` gains the optional `plate`. A test in `settings-panel-view`'s pure neighbour (or `settings-panel.test.ts`, over the zones) asserts every id is unique. The mirror gains a `<select>` copy per switch (stage 1, with the key fix, since its copies are what the keys land on).

**A new pure module, `src/toolkit/menus/logic/whip-pan.ts`** (beside `iso-view.ts`, which it builds on; tested in `whip-pan.test.ts`). It holds:

- `WHIP_SECONDS = 0.2` (the brief's 0.15 to 0.25 s; under half `ZOOM_SECONDS`).
- `whipEase(t)`: a strong ease in and out, quintic (`16t⁵` to the middle, mirrored), so the move is almost all in its middle third and stands still at either end; peak speed about 1.9 times the cubic's for the same distance and time, which with the shorter time reads as a flick.
- `whipBetween(from, to, t)`: as `viewBetween`, with `whipEase` for the centre and the scale (eased in proportion, as the zoom's), so the zoom and the whip share one view model.
- `plateOfTarget(target, layout)`: the module named by the target's `plate`, else the one containing its point, else null.
- `zoomedStep(targets, layout, focused, zoomedPlate, intent)`: what a direction, Tab or Shift-Tab means while zoomed. It asks `menuStep` (and through it `moveFocus`) for the next control, so the choice is the menu's own spatial rule across the whole panel, not only within the plate; and returns `{ kind: 'whip', id, plate }` when that control's plate differs from the zoomed one, `{ kind: 'focus', id }` when it is on the same plate (the focus moves, the camera does not), and `{ kind: 'none' }` when `moveFocus` stays put (nothing that way: nothing moves). Any other intent passes `menuStep`'s answer through unchanged.
- **Where the focus is not on the zoomed plate** (a click zoomed to a plate, or to a blank, which has no control; the focus null; or its id no longer among the targets after a rebuild): the first direction is taken from the zoomed plate's centre, as a virtual target, so it goes to the nearest control that way from what is on the screen, never from an old focus elsewhere and never `moveFocus`'s fall-back to the first in reading order (`menu-navigation.ts:147-148`). A click that zooms to a module also focuses that module's first control in reading order (shown only once a key or the pad is used, as now).
- **While zoomed, the four directions always move**, also on a knob or selector, which Left and Right turn unzoomed: zoomed in, the player is moving between controls, and turning stays on LB and RB, PageUp and PageDown, `-` and `+`, Enter and A (a step up), and a click on the knob's half. `menuStep` gains an option for this (`{ directionsTurn: false }`), rather than the whip module rewriting its answers. See the open question.
- `menuRepeatDue(lastAt, now)` and `MENU_REPEAT_SECONDS = STICK_REPEAT` (0.35 s): a held key's or d-pad's repeated direction is accepted only that long after the last accepted one, so a held key whips from control to control at the stick's pace instead of thirty times a second. A fresh press (not a repeat) is always accepted at once. Turns are not gated (holding Left on a knob unzoomed still sweeps it as now). The gate's clock is the last accepted focus move, whatever its source and whether zoomed or not (so a held key that crosses a zoom keeps its pace); a repeat that finds nothing that way does not reset it; a different direction is a fresh press.

**Interruption and retargeting.** Every accepted step starts a fresh whip from wherever the camera is now (`view`, mid-whip or mid-zoom included) to the new plate's `plateView`, with `t` from 0; nothing is queued. A press toward a control on the plate already being whipped to only moves the focus. Esc or B mid-whip zooms out from the present view, as now.

**Same-plate moves.** The focus ring and the mirror's focus move; `view`, `zoom` and `zoomed` are untouched; one redraw.

**Unzoomed, unchanged.** `menuStep` as now (Left and Right turn knobs; arrows, and now WASD, move the focus; the panel scrolls to the focus); no camera moves. The repeat gate also applies unzoomed to focus moves (a held arrow now walks the focus at the stick's pace rather than racing to the edge); see the open question.

**The pad.** The d-pad already arrives as `up`, `down`, `left`, `right`; `padIntents` gains a held d-pad repeat on the same `STICK_REPEAT` clock as the stick (today a held d-pad button moves once). The left stick's repeats are already at 0.35 s. Both reach the overlay as the keys do and whip the same way. `MenuListener.intent` gains a third argument, `repeat: boolean` (a keyboard `event.repeat`, a stick or d-pad repeat), for the gate; the title and plain-menu listeners ignore it.

**Mouse and touch.** No direction input exists. While zoomed, a press is tested as now, a control first (`pick`, `menu-overlay.ts:441-447`), then a plate (`plateAt`): a control on any plate on the screen is worked where it stands, as now, and the camera does not move; another plate's bare face (a module's or a blank's) whips to it rather than zooming at the zoom's pace (a small change in `zoomTo`, which takes the curve and duration); the zoomed plate's own face, or nothing (off the panel), does nothing, as now (the drag to scroll stays off while zoomed, `menu-overlay.ts:454`). A swipe while zoomed is not added (open question).

**The smear.** Optional and last (stage 4), only if cheap: an SVG `feGaussianBlur` with a horizontal or vertical deviation, applied by CSS `filter: url(#menu-smear)` to the overlay's canvas, its deviation set each frame from the whip's speed along its main axis (zero at rest, a few pixels at the peak), and removed when the whip ends. No extra render pass and no shader. It is measured with the overlay's own `stats` (frame time while whipping) on the MacBook Air and an iPad, and dropped if it costs a frame or looks wrong in Safari.

**The help line.** The overlay has no help line of its own today; the room's help line is hidden behind the menu (`first-person.ts:1552`, `help.hidden`), and the panel's controls card lists `BINDINGS` (`pause-menu.ts:69-96`). In `src/toolkit/controls/logic/controls.ts`, a `menuZoomHelp(mode)` gives the zoomed line on each input: keyboard and mouse "Arrows or WASD: next control · Enter: press · PageUp / PageDown: turn · Esc: zoom out", gamepad "D-pad or stick: next control · A: press · LB / RB: turn · B: zoom out", touch "Tap a control · tap another plate: go to it · back: zoom out"; `menuNavigate` and `BINDINGS`' last row gain WASD. The overlay shows the line in a small caption at the foot of the screen (the HUD's help-line style) while zoomed, chosen by the last input it heard: an intent `from` the keyboard gives the keyboard line, `from` the gamepad the pad's, a pointer press its `pointerType` (`mouse` gives the keyboard and mouse line, which name the same keys; `touch` and `pen` the touch line); opening by Esc starts on the keyboard line, by Start on the pad's, by the gear on the pointer's, with the mirror announcing it once per zoom (`aria-live="polite"`). `controls.test.ts` covers the four lines.

**Changes to `menu-overlay.ts`.**

- `zoom` gains its curve and duration: `{ from, to, t, seconds, between }`, so `frame` (`menu-overlay.ts:203-214`) advances `t` by `delta / zoom.seconds` and calls `zoom.between`; `zoomTo(box, how = 'zoom' | 'whip')`. `box === zoomed` becomes a box equality on `x`, `y`, `w`, `h`, so the same plate found twice is not zoomed to again.
- The intent handler (`menu-overlay.ts:516-549`): when `zoomed`, call `zoomedStep`; `whip` sets the focus and `zoomTo(plate, 'whip')`, `focus` calls `showFocus` without scrolling, `none` does nothing (and the key is still taken, so nothing reaches the game); repeats pass `menuRepeatDue` first. Unzoomed, as now.
- `showFocus` does not scroll while zoomed; zooming out (`zoomTo(null)`) first scrolls the whole-panel view to the focused control, so the panel comes back with it in view.
- The pointer's plate zoom focuses its module's control; a click on another plate while zoomed whips.
- The zoomed help caption, and its removal on close and resize.
- For checking (development only, binding no input): `menuPanel.zoomState()` returns `{ zoomed, view, kind, t }`, so a browser check can sample a whip frame by frame. The existing `menuIntent` handle delivers directions as a keyboard or pad would.

### Tests (pure)

- `whip-pan.test.ts`: `whipEase` is 0 and 1 at its ends, monotonic, symmetric, and steeper in the middle than `easeInOut`; `whipBetween` reaches `to` at 1 and keeps the scale between the two; `zoomedStep` on a small layout of three plates (one with two controls side by side, a knob among them): Right from the left control of the pair moves the focus only; Right from the right one whips to the next plate; Up where nothing is above returns `none`; Left on a zoomed knob moves rather than turns; Tab whips in reading order; a zoom on a blank takes the first direction from the blank's centre; `menuRepeatDue` accepts a fresh press at once and a repeat only after 0.35 s.
- `menu-navigation.test.ts`: `menuStep` with `directionsTurn: false`; `padIntents` repeating a held d-pad button.
- `controls.test.ts`: `menuZoomHelp` on each input; the help names WASD.

## Risks

- **Screen readers.** Taking the arrows on the mirror's range inputs changes what a keyboard-driven screen-reader user gets: Up and Down move between controls instead of stepping the slider, Left and Right turn it through the menu. Screen readers' own browse-mode commands do not send these keys to the page, so this should be the same for them; checked with VoiceOver in stage 1.
- **The repeat gate slows held arrows unzoomed.** A held arrow walks the focus at 0.35 s a step instead of the operating system's rate. It is the menu's own rate the brief asks for, but it changes how the unzoomed menu feels.
- **Directions on a zoomed knob no longer turn it.** A player zoomed on the field of view knob who presses Right expects it to turn; with this plan it whips away. Mitigated by the help line naming the turn keys; the owner may prefer otherwise (below).
- **WASD now move the title menus' focus too** (`title-overlay.ts` uses `menuStep`), and the plain menu's gamepad path. Probably wanted, but a change.
- **The smear in Safari:** CSS filters on a WebGL canvas can force a slow compositing path; hence measured and optional.
- **Shared checkout:** `menu-overlay.ts` may be claimed by the use-cue work; `TODO.md` and `CHANGELOG.md` are shared (stage only this mission's hunks).

## Open questions for the owner

1. While zoomed, should Left and Right on a knob or selector whip to the next control (this plan), or keep turning it, so that only Up and Down (and WASD) whip from a knob?
2. Should the keyboard's held-arrow repeat be gated to the menu's 0.35 s unzoomed too, or only while zoomed (leaving the unzoomed focus as quick as the operating system's repeat)?
3. Should W, A, S and D move the focus unzoomed as the arrows do (this plan; the brief says they already do, which they do not), and on the title's menus too?
4. While zoomed, should each whip frame its plate at that plate's own zoom (this plan, as a click would), or hold the present scale so it reads as a pure pan?
5. A help caption on the screen while zoomed (this plan), or only the controls card and the screen reader's announcement?
6. Touch: is a tap on another plate enough to move while zoomed, or is a swipe wanted as the touch twin of the arrows?
7. The smear: wanted at all, after seeing the plain whip?
