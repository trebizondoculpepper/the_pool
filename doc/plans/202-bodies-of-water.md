# 202: Bodies of water — working design plan

Brief: [202](../prompts/202-bodies-of-water.md). Drafted 2026-11-05. The owner asked for agent design review, then implementation. This is the design record before gameplay code changes.

## Existing seams

The pool halls describe rectangular and free-form basins in `logic/pool-halls.ts`. `basinFloorAt` returns the floor, ledge or step height at a point, and `hallFloorAt` already gives that height to the shared first-person controller; it currently makes the walker stand on a deep pool's floor with no swim state. The Jacuzzi has a separate raised tub in `plant/pool-amenities.ts` and no hall basin. The water surface is drawn in `pool-hall-room.ts` from the pool's saved `level` (or the dev room's level panel), using each room's `waterDepth`; that calculation needs one pure source shared by the visual and movement bindings. The pool edge is at deck height, with nominal water `WATER_Y = -0.015 m` (footbath full level is -0.03 m). The Jacuzzi derives its visual surface from `jacuzziVisualVolume` and a stepped tub fill profile, so its movement query must reuse that same conversion.

The shared walker lives in `src/toolkit/controls/input/first-person.ts`, takes `floorAt`, `solids` and `ladders` from the room, and already owns movement for every input device. Missions 175/181/182 derive walkable surfaces from geometry and traits; the new water volume must compose with that mechanism, not insert a room-name rule into controls. Existing `humanLadderZones` and basin steps supply deep-water exits. Water optics are mission 203's responsibility, and water appearance is mission 204's; mission 202 should expose the camera's water context to those systems rather than duplicate their shaders.

## Model and registry

Define a `water-body` primitive in a volume facet with a gameplay meaning: a walker inside this water can wade or swim. Register `pool`, `footbath` and `spa` kinds through `npm run -s audit -- register --traits` and mark the existing visual basins and tub with those kinds; the trait controls the behaviour, while each basin's outline, floor geometry and live level supply the volume. The Jacuzzi's `spa` node and the Lounge and Footbath schematic nodes are used where present. Keep tanks without the trait, so an open tank is not treated as a swimming pool. The registry command owns kind edits; no direct edits to kind files. A sunken architectural water volume has no floor/wall/ceiling mount, so the support facet exempts a water body without a physical form. This follows from the owner's request to implement the water-body trait and is covered by a focused trait test. The physical Jacuzzi tub remains a cylindrical, floor-standing kind.

The three short kind IDs follow the existing pool, footbath and spa schematic node names. Each hall object is explicitly bound to its kind. The bench catalogue counts these bindings as installed copies when no layout or inventory copy already exists, so the hall audit can discover the architectural basins. The Jacuzzi kind is solid as an exterior tub; the hall's interior water query supplies its walkable seat and floor, and the browser check confirmed entry and exit.

A generic read-only `WaterSample` query answers, at a world-space `(x, z)`: which water body contains the point, its current surface height and the basin-floor height below. Cache only the static outline and floor shape; read the renderer's live water plane on every movement step, after it has been updated from the world or the dev panel. The Jacuzzi tub's own live mesh is the corresponding surface source, including its dev fill control. Keep scene-unit conversion at the room boundary, so the shared pure movement rules use consistent scene units with a supplied metres-to-scene scale; test a scale other than 1. The floor may slope or include shelves, seats and steps; local depth is `surfaceY - floorY`, never a single basin-wide constant.

## Shared movement rule

Add a pure transition function for land, wading and swimming, driven by the volume query and the previous position. At depth up to 1.2 m, stand on the basin floor and wade at 0.9 m/s with the head above water. In deeper water, hold the eye 0.12 m above the live surface, move at 0.8 m/s, and add a small visual bob unless reduced motion is enabled. A short entry submersion may follow a permitted fall and then rise to the swim height in about one second, once the owner rules on high falls and mission 203's rendering hook is available; no diving, underwater steering, drowning or breath timer. Do not store a duplicate water level in the walker.

For a wader who can stand at the edge, permit a low clamber to the adjacent walkable deck through the same movement input. In deep water, permit exit only through a ladder or basin steps, using their existing zones or geometry. A swimmer reaching a ladder must mount at the water surface, not at the ladder's floor-level bottom exit. An empty or drained basin is a floor with no water state; a falling water level can transition a swimmer to wading when the local depth becomes shallow. At the drained deep end, the edge is blocked when its fall to the basin floor exceeds 1 m; at a partly drained edge, the water surface sets the drop. Room boundaries and dry corridors retain ordinary walking.

The current project rule stops drops over 1 m. The brief proposes an exception for a fall from a height into water, but that exception needs the owner's explicit ruling before implementation; ordinary deck-to-water entry is under 1 m. Keep high ledges blocked until that ruling is settled. A drained deep pool must block a deck entry regardless of the high-water-fall ruling, because there is no water to break the fall.

## Verification and integration

Pure cases: local depth over a slope, shelf, Jacuzzi seat and step; nominal and drained water levels; deck entry; shallow wade and all-edge exit; deep swim and ladder/step-only exit; partly drained edge; level falling while occupied; reduced-motion bob; no water in tanks; non-unit scene scale. Shared-controller cases: keyboard, touch and gamepad movement use the same state, and an ordinary dry floor still behaves as before. Browser checks: enter the Lounge, Olympic, Jacuzzi and Footbath water, leave by each permitted route, and inspect a changing level. Audit checks: every basin is a registered kind with `water-body`, tanks are not, and no unregistered pool geometry is reported. Coordinate any camera-underwater flag with mission 203 and sound attenuation with the game's audio owner before editing those systems.
