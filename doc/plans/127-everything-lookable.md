# 127: Everything lookable (plan and record)

Brief: [doc/prompts/127-everything-lookable.md](../prompts/127-everything-lookable.md). The owner said go on 2026-11-02. Scope as revised that day: the audit screens and `npm run look:audit` working; bringing the rooms up to code is mission [132](../prompts/132-bring-everything-up-to-code.md).

## The pieces

- **The shared classification** (`src/toolkit/gallery/logic/object-class.ts`, pure): `objectClass(key)` gives a kind, or a placed item's key, its class: `architectural` for signs (`sign`, `store-signs`) and fire fittings (`fire-point`, `fire-repeater`), then the owner's ruled decoration, then the kinds' register (`plant`, `non-plant`, `decorative`). `decorativeKind` (`world-in-model.ts`) is now its `decorative` test, so signs are no longer decoration; the model-binding checks exempt decoration and architectural components alike. `kindCatalogue` takes its classes from it: signs are class `architectural`, lookable (`expected` where registered, else `unverified`, never `none`), and not given the engineering findings `no-tag` and `no-maker`. 131's `classOf` (`src/game/descriptors/descriptor.ts`) still reads the register alone; it can call `objectClass` when 131 describes an architectural kind.
- **New marks** (`component-identity.ts`): `architectural` (a named, lookable thing outside the model, never flagged unbound) and `fabric` (walls, floors, ceilings, coves, openings: skipped by the audit, not lookable). `room-equipment.ts` marks placed signs and fire fittings by their label as architectural; the plant-room door template (`plant-door.ts`) marks itself `Plant room door` (or its plate's name) as architectural. `unboundInScene` exempts both marks; `lookOwners` skips fabric. No room's fabric is marked yet: that is 132's work, and the audit counts unmarked fabric as unlabelled until then.
- **The scanner** (`src/toolkit/gallery/views/look-audit-scene.ts`, shared with 129): one walk of a built room's scene for every marked owner (125's owners, plus its box and its architectural mark) and every mesh with no marked owner (with its nearest named ancestors and size), skipping the services ceiling and effects as set dressing by rule, and noting decorative marks on things named as signs. Then each target's front look, through 125's own `lookAt` (split out of `lookIn` in `look-scene.ts`, with a lazy option that stops at the first candidate `chooseLook` would take), and a ray along its front for a wall within 0.3 m. It also gathers what the data expects: the room's `lookCatalogue` entries (with the room's dev exhibits, loaded from `EXHIBITS`) and the register's (`INSTALLED_ASSET_IDS`), and 131's controls (`objectDescriptors(room)`).
- **The audit** (`src/toolkit/gallery/logic/look-audit.ts`, pure, tested): `lookTargets` (125's grouping: tag, else binding, a same-named piece folded into its whole), one `TargetRecord` per target (ref, tag, name, model binding, kind, class, constituent owners, named parts, catalogue status or `absent`, front look, size, findings, exception), and the findings: unlabelled groups (≥ 5 cm across, largest first by volume), unreachable expected entries and unverified ones apart, duplicate tags (owners of one tag bound to different model objects, or more than 1 m apart with nothing bridging them; a pipe and its markings are one), duplicate names (listed, reached by `#n`), bad fronts (under 5 of 9 rays, or a wall within 0.3 m), signs marked decorative, and 131's controls whose refs do not resolve as parts (the test 131 asked for: its keys against 127's marks). `KNOWN_UNLOOKABLE` (empty) excuses a finding by room and subject; `failing` counts the unexcused unlabelled, unreachable and duplicate-tag findings.
- **The room-build adapter** (`src/main.ts`, `?audit=look`): the game builds the room as any visit does, waits for its look hook's `settled` (or 3 s of frames for a room without one), scans it once, and leaves the audit on `window.__poolLookAudit`. Rooms are built by the game itself, so the audit sees what the player sees; nothing runs per frame.
- **The screens** (`bench.html?bench=lookables`, `src/toolkit/gallery/views/look-audit-page.ts`, development only): `&room=<id>` builds the room in a frame through the adapter and lists every finding and every target's record, each target a look link; without a room, every room's summary from the committed report, with a button that audits every room again in the page, one frame at a time. The screens label nothing partial: a blank or a count shows the gap (the owner's ruling).
- **The runner** (`npm run look:audit`, `scripts/look-audit.ts`): headless Chrome against the running dev server (it never starts one, as 125's `look.ts`), one fresh tab a room at `?room=<id>&audit=look&shot=1`; writes `doc/look-audit.json` (each room's counts, failing, timing, and its findings) and `doc/look-audit.md`; exits non-zero when any room fails or cannot be audited. Named rooms (`npm run look:audit -- plant_pool_03`) merge into the existing report. The owner allowed script aliases in `package.json` (2026-11-02): `look:audit` and 125's `look` are added.
