# A new game, perfectly normal

The owner's mission (2026-10-31): New Game (`newWorld` in `src/game/world.ts`) starts in a perfectly normal, clean, neutral state, and stays so for at least its first hour: clean, balanced water in every pool; clean filters; every balance tank at its working level; the backwash pit empty; every machine in its normal duty; no alarm, trip or inhibit. After the hour the poolside test kit (`test_kit`, `src/game/plant/test-kit.ts`) reads every pool normal, and Dr Aqualis (the Trios on the Lounge, PL1-CP2, and the Olympic, PL2-CP2) agrees: no alarm, warning or First Aid condition, every reading in its band, and no hand dose asked for. Anything the automatic dosing pumps can put right is fine; a new game needs no hand dosing.

The check is executable: `src/game/new-game-normal.slow.test.ts`, with its bands and checker in `src/game/new-game-normal.test-support.ts` (`abnormalities`). It checks the first frame, the first Trio reading (10 s), and every minute of the first hour run as the game runs it (`advance`, 0.1 s steps; about 4 s), and a negative control: the start a new game had before this mission is caught on each count. It builds on the healthy-start contract (`doc/healthy-start.md`, `src/game/health.ts`), whose faults it includes.

## How "normal" is judged

Every band is the game's own where it has one. The Trio pools (Lounge, Olympic) are judged by the Trio's commissioned targets (`DEFAULT_TARGETS`, `src/game/plant/aqualis-types.ts`); the spas, which have no Trio, by the healthy-start contract's bands (`HEALTHY`, `src/game/health.ts`), which sit inside the plant annunciator's limits (`src/toolkit/gallery/logic/world-reading.ts`). A test-kit reading is judged against its band widened by the kit's tolerance and half its step (`KIT`), since a water exactly at an edge may read either side of it.

Dr Aqualis is read through what his cabinet shows (`src/toolkit/gallery/views/aqualis-room-cabinet.ts`): the Trio's own readings, `trioAlarms`, its First Aid, inhibit and lockout, and `advise` on its readings with its working settings. The test asks for no advice above info, and no Show dose anywhere but `automatic`. The owner first asked that any recommended dose be very small (a few per cent of a plainly out-of-band correction), then relaxed it: the automatic pumps may do what they need to, and a new game must need no hand dose at all. So the threshold for a hand dose (bicarbonate, soda ash, calcium chloride, thiosulphate, dilution, a shock) is none; the pumps' duty is not limited. The Trio (at the balance tank) and the kit (at the pool's far side) must agree within the kit's tolerance on pH and alkalinity and twice it on free chlorine.

Where the model had no normal, normal UK practice (PWTAG) was taken: indoor pools carry no cyanuric acid, so the kit reads "below 15"; TDS no more than 1,000 mg/l over the mains' (350 mg/l), inside the Trio's 1,500.

## What was not normal, and what was changed

- **Alkalinity below its band in every pool.** A new game took its water from `SETTLED`, the bench's 120-day steady state with acid dosing and no bicarbonate ever added: 51 and 68 mg/l in the Trio pools, so Dr Aqualis warned "Alkalinity low" from the first reading and asked for 17 kg and 235 kg of bicarbonate by hand. Now the Trio pools start at 100 mg/l (`MAINTAINED_ALKALINITY`, `water-treatment.ts`), as operators keep them; `newWaterTreatment(pools, true)` is the new game's, and a migrated save still takes the settled water. 100, not the Trio's 120 set point, until mission 118 (2026-11-01) put the CO₂ loss right: now every pool, the spas too, starts at 120 (see the first two findings below).
- **pH at the edge of the Trio's band.** Pools started at 7.4, the Trio's high; they now start at 7.3, its and the old controller's set point.
- **Dirty filters.** The Olympic's beds started "mid-season" at 1.6 times their dirt reference, the Lounge's at 0.6; both now start clean (`CLEAN_BED_SHARE`, 0.05). A consequence: through clean beds the Olympic's unregulated pumps deliver some 675 m³/h, 9 % over its 620 design, until the beds load (recorded below).
- **Two day tanks below low.** Chosen in plan 085 so both transfer lines were seen to work at once; a new game now starts with every day tank 0.7 full and no line calling.

No world shape changed, so `WORLD_VERSION` is unchanged and existing saves keep their water.

Tests that had relied on the old start now set it up themselves: `onSettledWater` and `midSeasonFilters` in `src/game/world.test-support.ts` (the hand-dose bicarbonate test, the Trio-against-old-controller comparison, the backwash pit, the Olympic battery's design flow and tank draw-down, the filter descriptions); the chemical lines' calling and valves tests put a tank below low explicitly.

## Findings not fixed here

- **The Lounge's pH ran above the Trio's band after about 1.4 hours on water in band** (fixed, mission 118). At 100 mg/l alkalinity the Lounge's pH rose from 7.30 to 7.39 in the hour and to 7.40 after some 85 minutes, settling near 7.45. Two causes: the model's CO₂ loss (`co2LossConstant`, Wojtowicz's fit, quadratic in turnovers a day) taken far past the residential range it was fitted on, with his surface/volume misread, so the acid demand was several times what his data give; and the Trio's proportional-only pH control. The second was answered by the Trio's pH integral (2026-10-31); the first by holding the loss to his fitted range and correcting his reference (`doc/plans/aqualis-advice-verification.md`, "CO₂ loss reference cases" and "Mission 118: the CO₂ loss"). At alkalinity 120 the Lounge now holds 7.25–7.37 through its first day, its acid 18 l/day of 10 % (37 at 100 before).
- **The spas could not hold water in the alkalinity band** (fixed, mission 118). They degassed so fast (the same cause, at 68 and 160 turnovers a day) that only their settled 34 and 16 mg/l kept the pH in band, LSI −0.6 and −0.9. Now the Jacuzzi's still surface loses CO₂ at Wojtowicz's most-mixed rate (its jets and air add their own while they run), and both start at 120, LSI −0.05 and −0.07, holding pH 7.31–7.37 through the first day on their 0.5 and 0.1 l/h acid pumps (0.47 and 0.38 l/day used). Without bicarbonate their alkalinity drifts down (the Jacuzzi 18, the Footbath 39 mg/l on the first day, towards their settled 111 and 62); the test judges them on the Trio's alkalinity band and the LSI now, as the Trio pools.
- **The Olympic's acid pump is undersized** (the owner keeps it so, mission 101). On its old settled water at pH 7.4 it ran flat out from the first minutes, holding 7.40 at the Trio's edge; on alkalinity 100 from 7.3 its pH climbed slowly (7.31 at the hour, 7.34 at three hours) and the pump only started after some 2.7 hours. Since mission 118, at alkalinity 120, it delivers 136 l/day on the first day (5.7 l/h, three quarters of its 7.5) and holds 7.28–7.35.
- **The Olympic's flow through clean beds is 9 % over design**: its pumps have no regulating valve or speed control, so its circulation follows its filters' dirt (620 m³/h was the mid-season figure).
- **The Trios' first ten seconds.** Until its first tick (`CHEMISTRY_TICK`, 10 s) a Trio's pump status shows its channels unconnected and Dr Aqualis lists "No … pump connected" as information; the test checks the first frame for alarms and warnings only, and the channels online from the first reading.
- **`makeup-running`** flashes on the control room annunciator within the hour, as the booster set meets the pools' evaporation. It is ordinary activity (`INFORMATIONAL_INDICATIONS`) and the test allows it; whether it should be a window that needs accepting at all is the owner's call.
- Bathers arrive from 08:00 and a new game starts at Monday 00:00, so the hour is unloaded; days 2 and 3 with bathers (`aqualis-world.slow.test.ts`, on the settled water) take the Olympic's chlorine below the Trio's 1.0 at times (0.73 at the least), its hypochlorite pump undersized by the owner's choice.
