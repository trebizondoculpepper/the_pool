A working simulation

Pumps, filters, boilers, dosing and the controls that run them are modelled as working machines, not scenery. They have state and controls: gauges read real values, switches switch, lamps light. You can inspect them, operate them, work out what is wrong and put it right.

  1. Explore the plant rooms, corridors and control rooms behind the pools.
  2. Diagnose the problem from the instruments, the alarms and what you can see.
  3. Repair it, often in the room where it happened.
