The Jacuzzi plant room had kept a second hit test after the first shell-room migration in #46. Its `usableAt` walked from the first ray hit through the room parts, machines, cabinet services, fibre panel and Jacuzzi mimic, and the room passed separate click and aimed callbacks to its controller.

Most controls already registered their actions on their objects when their part sets were built. Both now register through the shared adapter and clean up on disposal (room-equipment disposal now includes its room-services view).
