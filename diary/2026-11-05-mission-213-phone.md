# Mission 213: the first phone view

The owner supplied Ilia_S's CC BY 4.0 handset. Its mesh was already modest at about 3,950 triangles, but its three 4096-pixel textures totalled roughly 25 MB. I kept the licensed source unchanged and made a shipped derivative with one 512-pixel colour map, simple rough shading and a black replacement for the separate front-glass mesh. The shipped glTF and assets are about 235 KB.

The handset is loaded when first raised, drawn over the room near the camera and eased into view. The owner checked the live dev view and guided two size and position adjustments, then approved the framing. M, D-pad Up and the touch phone icon toggle it. The game continues stepping while movement and use input are held; Escape lowers it before the settings menu. A carried item is hidden without being released and returns when the phone closes. Mission 214 can replace the black screen and add its menus.
