# Research: model speed, trait facets and object descriptions

The owner asked for the open research tasks. I investigated mission 042 and GitHub issues #43 and #44. The registry audit found 124 kinds and one actionable handling gap, `office-shelf`; other empty facets are mostly valid absences or service information derived from ports. The object-description audit found good existing identity and live-status seams but no single provenanced fact selector. Both issue reports recommend designs without altering traits or simulation behaviour.

For mission 042 I measured the current world and profiled its step. The old footbath tag-walk cost has largely been removed by an identified-block index. Repeated valve searches are now a promising small target. A scratch indexed/precompiled lookup produced the same outputs in sampled state toggles and large isolated timing gains, but I did not turn that into a production change. Larger catch-up steps were much faster but changed readings and have not been checked for missed fault events. The full mission's compiled-world, worker and connected 5× experiments remain open. See `doc/research/042-world-model-speed.md` for the figures, limits and ranked next steps.
