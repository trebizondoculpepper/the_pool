# Mission 176, strand 2: the operable ruling and the finishing classification

I took mission 176 back from farhad after the owner reassigned it, and its first strand — the support rule and the kind-traits enforcement — landed as `3e02c204`. A kind that sits in or ends a run, is held, or is itself a walk-through run now names no floor/wall/ceiling support, so `unclassified` went empty; `validateRegistry` fails a traitless kind and `register` requires `--traits`.

The rest was a long ruling conversation with the owner, recorded in `doc/plans/176-completion-rulings.md`. The important one: `operable` means a control whose action changes something else in the simulation, not merely a thing with a button. A passive heat exchanger, a sand or cartridge filter, the desk, the alarm printer and a display-only mimic all stopped claiming it; a UV unit keeps it for its own on/off switch; a valve stays operable because it changes the flow. `container` was redefined as "can hold other game objects" (a sand filter holds sand, but grains are not objects, so it is not a container). `panel-like` was pinned to thin things with no thickness worth simulating.

Three marker traits were added: `openable` (a door gives access, a local state), `slidable` (a thing that moves along a line — a fixed thing cannot slide, so it lives in handling beside fixed), and `safety-gear` (exists for realism, not simulated). Named composites `desk`, `mimic` and `safety-shower` replaced the generic furniture family where the owner asked for the thing's own name. Strand 2 landed as `aa25a909` and `8dd97e4c`, then the mission was filed complete in `doc/prompts/done/`.

Separately, the owner found a spot in `plant_pool_01` where the walker could not pass: the balance tank's overflow and main-drain pipes ran at 2.0 m across the walkway, their Ø355 underside at 1.82 m, just below the pipe-collision rule's 1.9 m walk-under threshold. Collision is 2D, so crouching never helped. The fix was the owner's: route the pipes in above the tank and drop them straight down into roof inlets. Their tank ports moved from the north face to the roof, facing up, and the runs now rise to 2.65 m, cross, and fall to 2.30 m — the walkway is clear (0 obstacles at the reported position). Committed as `8a67e2c5`.
