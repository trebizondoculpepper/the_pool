# Dev71’s first room trait, 2026-11-07

The owner chose dev71 as proof that a room can declare reusable behavior: two phone texts remain a deliberately small demonstration, while scenarios will use the same timer and trigger component for richer sequences. Mission 231 (#105) attaches `timed-events` through the audit CLI, injects phone delivery at the room-trait host boundary, and removes the dev71-only script and generic room-script hook. The trait’s clock is stepped by simulation time and its per-visit instance resets on reentry. A draft review is on PR #117.
