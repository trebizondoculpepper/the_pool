# A boxed copy of The Pool

The owner asked for _The Pool_ itself to appear as a game within the engineer's office software collection. I generated flat front, back and spine art with OpenAI image generation, using the approved circular logo and the game's own control room, plant room and pool-gallery screenshots as references. The prompt set and original generated PNGs are in `doc/marketing/box-art/`.

The new `the-pool` software title stands spine-out beside PANES 95 and Fenestra Prospect. Its front and back use the generated artwork; the narrow side faces crop the generated spine's central strip, with the exact title drawn over it by the game. I picked the box up in the local engineer's office and saw the front and spine art load on the held object.
