# Wet hall mimic research, 2026-11-01

The owner asked for a fresh-water destination mimic in place of WH-MP1 and a mirror dirty-water source mimic, each with four pool branch rates. They chose eight modeled branch flowmeters and pointed to a new location for the fresh board on the clean bay's west partition. The dirty board must make clear that Jacuzzi and Footbath drain-downs enter the pit before pumped discharge to sewer.

The pool water step already computes each pool's actual make-up, backwash and drain-down volumes, but only returns the per-pool make-up and aggregates pit inflow. The current intake flowmeter is before the break tank, and the outfall monitor after the pit; neither can supply per-pool rates. More significantly, Lounge and Olympic backwash join in the corridor, and the Jacuzzi and Footbath schematics still send drain and wash pipes to local gullies while the simulation adds their volumes to the pit. Mission 119 therefore calls for branch sensors before joins and a correction of the spa waste connections before the new boards claim those routes.

The brief and plan were drafted in `doc/prompts/119-wet-hall-water-mimics.md` and `doc/plans/119-wet-hall-water-mimics.md`. Review requests went to Nicole, Ahnaa and Farhad through agent-inbox. Ahnaa's checkout is on another machine, so the draft needs to be committed and pushed before she can inspect it. Implementation has not started.

Nicole and Ahnaa's early review confirmed the missing spa-to-pit physical links and found further accounting gaps: Lounge's pit waste includes dump, rinse and drains; Olympic and Jacuzzi tank drains currently enter `spilt` instead of either outlet; generic daily backwash can create a one-step flow spike if presented as an instantaneous rate. The mission and plan were amended to require truthful source indicators, timed waste flow, sensor damping and a check on the shared 160 mm main. Nicole reserved WORLD_VERSION 67 for the parallel completion of mission 118, so mission 119 must take the next free version if it persists sensor state.
